FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Choose a service in your dashboard, select M-Pesa, enter your Safaricom number and confirm the STK prompt on your phone.

Is registration free?

Yes. Creating a PesaView account is free. Service charges are applied when you purchase a package or request a paid service.

Can I see my transactions?

Yes. The dashboard includes a transaction history showing deposits, service purchases and payment status.

Can an administrator change prices?

Yes. The planned backend includes an admin panel for service packages, users and transactions.
